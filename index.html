Civic mail merge

Write Your Officials

Pick a place. We find who represents it, from city council to Congress. You write one message, and it goes to each of them separately from your own Gmail.

1

Where?

A full street address gives the most accurate districts. A city name works too, but state and congressional districts may be approximate.

3

Your message

Placeholders: {{name}} official's name, {{title}} their office, {{place}} your location. Each person gets their own copy, so nobody sees the others' addresses.

4

Send

    Your Gmail sign-in stays in your browser. This site has no server and stores nothing about you. Officials' data comes from the U.S. Census, the unitedstates/congress-legislators project, OpenStreetMap, Open States and Cicero when keys are set, and a community directory. Always double-check recipients before sending.
